SessionDock 2.7.3

Keyboard focus is now visible on selection controls

- Closed account, language, sound, batch, and history selectors now show a clear focus ring when reached with the keyboard.
- The focus indicator follows the active dark, light, or Windows high-contrast theme.
- A regression contract now protects every production selection control that inherits the shared ComboBox style.
- HandleScope remains optional and pinned to v0.1.3; its confirmed installation runs as the standard user and does not enable the SessionDock integration automatically.
